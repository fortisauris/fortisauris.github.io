SPACE FOR GROWTH

Information technology is everywhere we look today. Even the most resistant opponents have succumbed to tablets, smartphones, and social networks. Life without messengers, assistants, apps, or the web is as unimaginable for us as mandatory school attendance before the discovery of America.

But where do these miraculous things come from? How do they arise, and how do they work?

We have created a space that will provide you with answers to all your curious questions. Our HACKERSPACE is designed for curious minds who like to take things apart to understand how they work.

What You'll Learn

Linux Basics

Master computer control through the command line and learn the fundamentals of Linux systems.

Python Programming

Learn the popular programming language Python and build your first applications.

Web Development

Discover the basics of web design and create your own websites from scratch.

Advanced Opportunities
