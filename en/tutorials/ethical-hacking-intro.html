Lesson 0 - ETHICAL HACKING: What You Should Know

ETHICAL HACKING

Just as there are good people and bad people in the world, the same applies to hackers. Hackers who think only of personal gain and break into systems to steal and destroy are called BLACK HAT hackers. They have lent their abilities to malicious purposes and don't care about others.

The second group consists of hackers who, on the contrary, try to protect and secure every system against unauthorized access and abuse. If they discover a vulnerability or pathway into a system, they report it to the system owner and give them sufficient time to fix the issue. These hackers are called WHITE HAT hackers. They work as independent consultants or so-called PENETRATION TESTERS, who attempt to break into systems from the outside to reveal their inadequate security.

FORTIS AURIS HACKERSPACE RULES

FORTIS AURIS is 100% dedicated to public education in computer security and war-gaming only on our own hardware and software. We support Open Source, Open Education, Open Hardware because we believe that privacy protection, human rights, and freedoms should be a given in the 21st century.

  1. Fun and Safety First: What we do, we do for fun and we don't want to hurt or harm anyone.
  2. Legal and Consensual: We do everything with the consent of all parties involved and in accordance with Slovak Republic legal regulations.
  3. Inclusive Community: Every person of good will is welcome.
  4. Respectful Learning: All opinions are welcome, not all are necessarily accepted... we learn from better people to become better.
  5. Educational Purpose: We share knowledge freely and support each other's growth.

What Makes a Good Ethical Hacker?

Curiosity

Always asking "How does this work?" and "What if I try this?"

Ethics

Understanding the responsibility that comes with knowledge and power.

Technical Skills

Mastering programming, networking, and system administration.

Communication

Ability to explain technical issues to non-technical people.
